List the specific tells that give this draft away as not written by this person. Look for:
- phrasing that sounds reworded from the notes rather than typed off the cuff
- assistant polish: tidy parallel structure, stock or managerial phrases, over-precise nouns, filler transitions, a neat closer
- too tidy: perfect punctuation, capitalization and complete sentences where the real ones are looser (this person's measured imperfection level: {imperfection})
- signature habits overdone: a habit that appears in only some real messages copied into this one, or several habits stacked in one message
- word choice, openers, punctuation, capitalization, length and sentence shape that clash with how this person writes in {medium}
- anything longer, more complete or more explained than their real messages (their median is {median} words)

Rules for your critique:
- Judge style only. The content in the notes is fixed: never suggest adding reasons, reassurance, next steps, offers, sign-offs or details that aren't in the notes. Cutting is often the right fix.
- A few samples don't show everything this person does. Don't claim they never use a word or habit just because it's missing from these samples. Flag only what clearly clashes with them or with how people actually write in {medium}.
- Never suggest adding typos or mistakes; only point out polish the real messages don't have.
- Quote the exact offending words and say briefly what this person would more likely write instead.
- At most 4 tells, most important first. If the draft already reads like them, say "No clear tells." Don't write a revised version.
